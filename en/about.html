About Brothrone

Brothrone is a personal travel journal about flights, hotels, and the places in between.

I share first-hand flight reports, hotel and ryokan stays, and practical notes from planning each trip. Photos and honest observations are at the heart of every story.

Stories are available in Korean and English. For questions or collaboration enquiries, visit the contact page.
