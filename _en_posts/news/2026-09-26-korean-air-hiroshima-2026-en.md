Korean Air will fly **daily between Seoul Incheon and Hiroshima from December 23**. It is the first time Korean Air has operated the route itself, and the launch lands just before Christmas, in time for year-end trips to western Japan.

## Timetable

From [Korean Air's announcement of August 24](https://news.koreanair.com/%EB%8C%80%ED%95%9C%ED%95%AD%EA%B3%B5-%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%ED%9E%88%EB%A1%9C%EC%8B%9C%EB%A7%88-%EB%85%B8%EC%84%A0-%EC%8B%A0%EA%B7%9C-%EC%B7%A8%ED%95%AD-12%EC%9B%94-23%EC%9D%BC%EB%B6%80%ED%84%B0/) (in Korean). Times are local; Korea and Japan share the same time zone.

| Route | Departs | Arrives | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Incheon → Hiroshima | 10:35 | 12:15 | Daily |
| Hiroshima → Incheon | 13:25 | 15:25 | Daily |

The aircraft is the A321neo, the single-aisle type also used on the Incheon–Oita route that resumes on October 25, with Prestige and economy cabins.

A mid-morning departure from Incheon is reachable on an early train from other Korean cities. The return leaves Hiroshima at 13:25, so the last day is a morning checkout and straight to the airport.

## Around Hiroshima

Korean Air's announcement highlights Itsukushima Shrine on Miyajima, the Peace Memorial Park, the Shukkeien garden in the city centre and hot spring towns such as Tomonoura. Miyajima and the Peace Memorial Park are often combined in a single day.

Hiroshima is on the Sanyo Shinkansen line, which makes it easy to combine with other cities in the Chugoku region such as Okayama. Korean Air says it has also increased Incheon–Okayama to four flights a week, so flying into Hiroshima and out of Okayama is one option. Check on the booking screen whether both can go on one ticket and what it costs.

## From the airport

Hiroshima Airport sits on high ground well outside the city centre. Most travellers take the airport limousine bus into town; check the timetable and journey time on the airport's official site. With a 12:15 arrival, there is still time for an afternoon plan on day one.

## Before booking

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Round-trip total over the holidays | The first flight on December 23 lands in peak Christmas and New Year demand |
| Tax-free shopping from November 1 | Japan now refunds tax at departure; at Hiroshima Airport too, check before you drop bags |
| Flight number and seat | Bookings made around the December 17 Asiana integration may show changes |
| Airport bus times | Make sure a bus fits your arrival and departure |

Japan's tax-free change is covered in our [November 1 tax-free article](/en/news/japan-tax-free-refund-2026/). This checklist is practical advice, not airline policy.
