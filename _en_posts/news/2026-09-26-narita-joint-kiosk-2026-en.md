Arriving at Narita just got a little quicker.
**Since September 24, Narita Terminals 1 and 2 have "joint kiosks".**

Until now you showed your passport at immigration and again at customs.
These kiosks take **the information for both in one go.**

## What changes

From the [September 18 announcement by Japan Customs and the Immigration Services Agency](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/20260918.html) (in Japanese):

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start | September 24, 2026 |
| Where | Narita Terminals 1 and 2 |
| Number | 177 kiosks |
| You need | Visit Japan Web QR code + e-passport |
| Submitted at once | Passport details, face photo, customs declaration |

Panasonic Connect, which supplied the 177 kiosks, [announced](https://news.panasonic.com/jp/press/jn260918-2) the rollout the same day.

## Airports that already have them

Narita T1 and T2 are the latest additions. These were already running:

| Airport | Terminals |
| --- | --- |
| Haneda | Terminals 2 and 3 |
| Kansai | Terminals 1 and 2 |
| Narita | Terminal 3 (now also 1 and 2) |
| Fukuoka | International |

That covers almost every major Japanese airport people fly into from Korea.

## How it works for visitors

1. At the **joint kiosk**, scan your Visit Japan Web QR code and passport and follow the screen
2. Go to the **dedicated immigration booth for kiosk users**
3. Collect bags; if customs does not need to inspect you, **walk through with face recognition**

One thing to be clear on:
**the kiosk does not replace immigration.**
You still see an immigration officer, and customs may still check your bags.
But you show your passport fewer times and use a dedicated booth, so the whole process is shorter.

## Before you fly

Most important: **finish your Visit Japan Web registration in advance.**
No QR code, no kiosk.

If the app's arrival screen shows **"Immigration and customs declaration" as not registered**, there is no QR code yet.
Fill that part in and the code for the kiosk appears.

I have a step-by-step guide with screenshots:
→ [How to register on Visit Japan Web](/en/tips/visit-japan-web/)

You will need to open the QR code on arrival, so make sure your phone is charged and has data (SIM, eSIM or roaming).
