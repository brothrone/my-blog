Asiana has opened selected award flights to Narita, Osaka, Beijing, Hanoi and Manila. This is a release of award inventory on specified flights, not a blanket mileage discount or a promise of seats on every date.

![Asiana award flights: booking September 30 to December 10; selected travel dates November 3 to December 10, 2026](/assets/images/news/asiana-award-special-flights/booking-window.png)
*Original chart based on Asiana’s published dates · Brothrone Travel*

## Booking and travel windows

| Window | Dates in 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Booking | September 30–December 10 |
| Travel | Selected dates November 3–December 10 |
| Routes | Selected flights between Incheon and NRT, KIX, PEK, HAN or MNL |

Check the outbound and return separately before arranging accommodation. Having an eligible departure date does not mean the return you want is also included.

These combinations are our planning examples drawn from the published schedule, not live availability results.

Beijing’s weekday schedule runs November 9–27, excluding November 20 and 24. Consult the official table for all dates, including Manila.

## Checks before paying

Taxes and fuel surcharges are payable separately. Compare the cash portion with a regular paid ticket for the same travel period before deciding how to use your miles.

Award seats can sell out, and paid seats may be sold alongside them. Schedules and aircraft can change. Recheck the flight number and dates in the booking flow rather than relying only on the event table.

For a family trip, one available seat is not enough. Confirm the required number of seats in both directions and the cancellation terms before committing to accommodation.
