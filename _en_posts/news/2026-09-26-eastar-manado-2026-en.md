Manado might not ring a bell.
It sits at the northern tip of Sulawesi in Indonesia and is **well known among divers and snorkellers**.

Eastar Jet has **switched its Seoul Incheon–Manado route to scheduled service from September.**
When it launched last October it was a charter, with tour operators buying up every seat. **Now anyone can book directly on Eastar's website or app.**

## What changed

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type of service | Charter → **scheduled** |
| Frequency | **7 a week, daily** |
| Booking | Tour packages only → **direct booking on website and app** |
| Load factor | About 93% on average, May to September this year (per Eastar Jet) |

Eastar received traffic rights for the route from Korea's transport ministry in April and has been preparing the switch since.
It was the first Korean airline to fly Incheon–Manado and is still the only one. (As reported by [Economy Science](https://www.e-science.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=135903), [Seoul Economic Daily](https://m.sedaily.com/article/20093841) and others.)

## Timetable

| Route | Departs |
| --- | --- |
| Incheon → Manado | 20:25 |
| Manado → Incheon | 02:20 (local) |

Flying time is about 5 hours 30 minutes.
Manado is an hour behind Korea, so by that math **the outbound lands after midnight local time** and **the return reaches Incheon in the morning**.
Check the exact arrival times on the booking screen.

With a middle-of-the-night arrival, **book a first-night hotel that offers airport pickup**.
The return leaves at 2 am, so you also need to fill the last evening.
It is worth asking your hotel about late checkout in advance.

## Fares

To mark the switch, **a sale opened at 2 pm on September 22**.
**One-way fares with free checked baggage start at ₩168,100**, and there is a prize draw for local hotel stays and activity passes.

Sale seats are limited, so prices vary by date.
The fuel surcharge went up for tickets issued from October 1, so check the final total before paying. ([fuel surcharge post](/en/news/fuel-surcharge-october-2026/))

The big draw is **Bunaken National Marine Park**.
According to Eastar, around 400 species of coral and fish live there.
The usual plan is a boat out from the city for snorkelling or diving.

Away from the sea there is **trekking on Mount Mahawu** and **Tangkoko National Park**.
Tangkoko is known for tarsiers, among the smallest primates in the world.
The average temperature is around 26°C all year, which makes it a good escape from the Korean winter.

## Entering Indonesia

South Korean passport holders cannot enter Indonesia visa-free. **You need a visa on arrival (VOA).**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| VOA fee | **IDR 500,000** |
| Stay | 30 days |
| Getting it in advance | Apply online for an electronic visa on arrival (e-VOA) before you fly |
| Arrival card | Free on **All Indonesia** (allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id), from 72 hours before arrival |

The paper arrival card has been replaced by the All Indonesia online declaration.
It covers customs too, and you can apply for the e-VOA in the same process.

Rather than queueing at the airport in the middle of the night, **get the e-VOA before you leave.**
Apply only on the official Indonesian immigration site (an imigrasi.go.id address). Plenty of look-alike agency sites show up in search results and charge extra.

Your **passport also needs at least six months of validity** left.
