Korean Air's inflight Wi-Fi **started switching to Starlink, for free, on September 15.**

When I flew a Korean Air A350 in July 2025, Wi-Fi was paid: $4.95 for messaging, $5.95 for internet ([that flight review](/en/airline-review/ke787-icn-fuk/)).
So this is welcome news. It just is not on **every aircraft** yet.

## Which aircraft have it

Based on [Korean Air's September 11 announcement](https://news.koreanair.com/대한항공-9월-15일부터-스타링크-기반-기내-와이파이-서/) (in Korean):

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Start | September 15, 2026 |
| Aircraft | Three modified A350-900s and one 777-300ER |
| Price | Free in every cabin on those aircraft |
| Routes | Mainly medium and long haul to the Americas, Europe and Southeast Asia, plus some short haul |
| Not allowed | Internet voice and video calls, live streaming |
| Rollout | Most of the fleet by the end of 2027 |

At launch it is just **four aircraft**.
So "it's an A350, it'll have Starlink" does not work. Most aircraft of the same type have not been modified yet.
(The aircraft photos below are type references, not Starlink-equipped planes.)

Korean Air says most of its fleet will have Starlink by the end of 2027 ([One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/news/korean-air-free-starlink-wi-fi/)).
Until then, the same route can have it one day and not the next.

## Before you count on it

<img src="/assets/images/news/korean-air-starlink/777-exterior.jpg" alt="Korean Air 777-300ER at Incheon, 2012." loading="lazy" style="width:100%;height:auto" />

If you plan to work on a laptop or message someone before landing, have a backup.
Anything that really needs a connection is **best finished before you board.**

The aircraft type on your booking does not tell you whether that particular plane has Starlink.
Check the airline's information and follow the crew's instructions on board.

## Before you fly

| Prepare | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Save your e-ticket and hotel address | So you can see them offline |
| Download documents and shows | Your plane may not have Wi-Fi |
| Tell people when you will be reachable | So you do not promise replies mid-flight |

## Free in economy, but no calls

<img src="/assets/images/news/korean-air-starlink/777-cabin.jpg" alt="Korean Air 777-300ER economy, 2022." loading="lazy" style="width:100%;height:auto" />

The best part is that economy is free too.
But internet access **does not mean calls or video meetings.**
Think messages and email, and plan calls for the ground.
