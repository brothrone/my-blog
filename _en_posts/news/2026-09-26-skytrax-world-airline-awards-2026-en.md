The "world's best airline" headlines come round every year.
**Skytrax, the UK airline rating company, announced its 2026 World Airline Awards in London on September 18.**

This year's winner is **Singapore Airlines**.
Last year's champion, Qatar Airways, dropped to second, and this is the sixth time Singapore Airlines has taken the top spot.

## Korean Air stays in the top 10, but lower

Some Korean coverage described Korean Air as "entering the top 10". The official table shows it **fell three places, from 7th last year to 10th**.
It did stay in the top 10.

**Asiana Airlines and Korea's low-cost carriers are not in this year's top 100.**
Elsewhere, Thai Airways jumped from 29th to 20th, and Taiwan's STARLUX rose from 18th to 13th.

## How the ranking works

Skytrax ranks airlines from a **passenger survey**.
It ran online from September 2025 to August 2026, and more than 300 airlines are in the results. The survey was also offered in Korean.

So this is a **passenger satisfaction ranking**, not a safety or punctuality ranking.
Seats and service can vary a lot within one airline by route and aircraft, so check the aircraft type and seat layout of your actual flight when you book.
