Plenty of Korean travellers start their hotel search on Naver.
**Airbnb listings now appear there alongside hotels.**

Airbnb said on September 28 that it had **launched a search integration with Naver**.
Naver Hotels also has a new **"Shared stays" (공유숙박) tab**.

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Search | Search Naver for a destination plus a stay type, such as **"Osaka stays" (오사카 숙소)** |
| 2. Results | **Airbnb listings appear alongside** hotels and other options |
| 3. See more | Opens the Naver Travel hotel tab; the **"Shared stays" tab** shows Airbnb listings only |
| 4. Details | Photos, amenities, reviews, **live prices and availability** |
| 5. Booking | **You are sent to Airbnb to pay** |

Searching for shared-stay keywords brings up Airbnb listings only and links straight to the Shared stays tab.
(As reported by [Travel Daily](https://www.traveldaily.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=72213) and [Travel Times](https://www.traveltimes.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=500470).)

## Not every listing appears

Two things to know:

**1. Overseas listings only.**
Airbnb stays in Korea are not part of this integration. For Jeju or Gangneung, you still need the Airbnb app.

**2. Only listings that meet certain criteria.**
According to Airbnb, only **overseas listings that meet standards such as high guest ratings** appear on Naver.
The **"Guest favourite" badge**, based on ratings, reviews and reliability, is shown too.

So if a place does not show up on Naver, that does not mean there are no Airbnbs in the area.
To see everything, check the Airbnb app as well.

## What to compare

Having hotels and Airbnbs on one screen makes comparing easier, but **compare like with like**.

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Final total | Airbnb stays can add cleaning and service fees, so the nightly rate alone can mislead |
| Price basis | Hotels price per room; Airbnbs are often per home. For families or groups, **compare the total for everyone** |
| What's included | Breakfast, daily cleaning, a front desk to hold bags |
| Cancellation | Policies vary by listing |

Since the booking happens on Airbnb, **check the total and the cancellation policy on Airbnb before paying**.
Airbnb says it also accepts Kakao Pay and Naver Pay.
