A trip to Thailand looks set to cost a little more.
**The Thai government is again pushing a 450-baht (about US$15) fee for every foreign tourist.**

Nothing is final yet.
The plan is still out for consultation and **needs Cabinet approval before it can take effect**.
But the government's target is **the first quarter of 2027, starting with air arrivals**, so it is worth knowing about if you are planning a trip early next year.

## What we know so far

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | **450 baht per person**, whatever the way you arrive |
| Who pays | Foreign tourists |
| Target start | **Q1 2027, air arrivals first** |
| Land and sea arrivals | Phased in about 360 days later |
| Validity | **Pay once, then enter and leave freely for 30 days** |
| Use of funds | Tourist insurance (safety and health), destination development and more |
| Current stage | **Public hearing until September 28** → committee review → Cabinet approval |

This is based on Thai reporting including [The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40070950).
The National Tourism Policy Committee approved the draft on August 14, and **collection starts 180 days after the notice is published in the Royal Gazette**.

So even on the fastest timeline, it will be **at least six months after publication** before anyone pays it.
Winter trips you are booking now should not be affected.

## It used to be 300 baht

The earlier plan charged **300 baht for air arrivals and 150 baht by land or sea**.
The new plan sets one 450-baht rate for everyone. For travellers arriving by air, that is **150 baht more** than before.

According to the tourism ministry, a calculation based on current prices and costs came out above 490 baht, but the rate was cut to 450 baht to keep the burden reasonable.

## How you would pay

That is still undecided.
Options under consideration include **adding it to the ticket price, paying on a website or app, and kiosks at the airport**.

If it goes into the ticket price, there is nothing to do. If it is paid separately, **you may need to pay online before you arrive**.
Once it is confirmed, I will cover how to pay.

## Airport charges have already gone up

Separately, **Thailand's international departure charge (PSC) already rose on June 20.**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | **730 baht → 1,120 baht** |
| Airports | Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang, Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai, Phuket and Hat Yai (the six AOT airports) |
| Applies to | Tickets issued from June 20 |
| How | Included in the ticket price |
| Domestic flights | Unchanged at 130 baht |

Source: [TAT Newsroom](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/05/airports-of-thailand-to-raise-international-passenger-service-charge-from-20-june-2026/).
It is already in your fare, so it is easy to miss, but it explains why taxes on Thailand return tickets have crept up.

If the tourist fee goes ahead, **flying in and out would mean 1,120 baht in airport charges plus the 450-baht fee, 1,570 baht in total**.
