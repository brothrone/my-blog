## When the transfer happens

The announced process covers **existing Asiana reservations and purchased tickets for departures from December 17, 2026 onward**. Transfers are scheduled to take place in stages from **November 2 to December 3**.

Affected customers will receive individual notices through Kakao notifications, text messages or email. [Korean Air's announcement, in Korean](https://news.koreanair.com/대한항공-통합-항공사-출범-준비-본격화-아시아나항/)

## What to check when the notice arrives

Start with the phone number and email address attached to your booking. If you booked through an agency, ask where the update will be sent.

Once you receive it, check the flight number, departure date and time in the new reservation. Open the booking again before heading to the airport instead of relying only on a screenshot saved months earlier.

The announcement does not explain how selected seats, prepaid baggage or special meals will be handled. If you arranged any of these, check that they appear in the updated booking and contact the seller if something is missing. A flight-number change notice alone doesn't confirm the details of those services.

If the published eligibility and your individual booking don't match, check with the airline or the agency that issued your ticket.
