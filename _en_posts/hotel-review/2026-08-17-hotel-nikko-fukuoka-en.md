Today I'm writing up **Hotel Nikko Fukuoka**, a short walk from Hakata Station.
I stayed one night here after wrapping up a trip around Saga, right before flying home. Hakata Station is about 10 minutes on foot, which made getting to the airport the next morning painless.

## Exterior

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka exterior at night](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/exterior.webp)

The exterior, shot when we got in late at night. It's right on a main road so you won't have trouble finding it, and there's a bus stop out front.

## Lobby

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka lobby](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/lobby.webp)

The lobby is huge. High ceilings, marble floors — it's a little overwhelming the moment you walk in.

There's a seating area off to one side of the lobby. The windows are big and look straight out to the street, so it's a nice place to sit while you wait for check-in.

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka guest room corridor](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/room.webp)

The corridor leading to the rooms.

We booked two rooms this time — one **double**,

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka twin room](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/room3.webp)

and the other was a **twin**.

The rooms were spotless. Not huge, but everything you need is there — and the amenities were **LA MER**, which caught me off guard.

## Bathroom

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka bathroom](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/bath.webp)

The bathroom was clean too. It shows its age a bit, but there's a proper tub — plenty good enough for a soak on the last night of the trip.

## View

![View toward Hakata Station from Hotel Nikko Fukuoka](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/view.webp)

The room window looks out toward Hakata Station. As you can see, it's genuinely right there — an easy walk even dragging a suitcase.

## Breakfast

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka breakfast buffet](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/breakfast.webp)

Breakfast was really good.

![Hotel Nikko Fukuoka breakfast plate](/assets/images/hotel-review/hotel-nikko-fukuoka/breakfast2.webp)

## Verdict

Location is what makes **Hotel Nikko Fukuoka** worth it. Hakata Station is a 10-minute walk, the airport run is easy, and there's plenty of shopping and food within walking distance.
The hotel itself is big and well kept, and nothing about the stay bothered us.

The one catch is the **price — it isn't cheap**.
Tenjin and Nakasu are a bit further out, but the subway runs straight there, so it never really got in the way.

If you're **after a hotel near Hakata Station** — especially when **an early flight means you need to be close to the airport** — this is an easy one to recommend.
